TELND — Career & Talent Platform   Page 1  TELND  Complete Product Features, Workflow & Development Timeline  Positioning:   TELND is a career and talent ecosystem that helps people prepare for careers, discover opportunities, prove their skills, get hired, and continue growing.  Employer value:   TELND helps companies discover, assess, interview, hire, and eventually outsource talent. AI Career Assistant  • Natural-language job search that actually performs platform actions rather than behaving as a generic chatbot.  • Examples: 'Find me a marketing job in Chattogram under   n 40,000' or 'Apply to the first suitable job.'  • AI can search jobs, explain matches, track applications, improve CVs, generate cover letters, prepare interviews, recommend courses, identify skill gaps, provide salary guidance and send reminders.  • AI should have controlled access to TELND backend tools and user data, with permissions and audit logs.  5. Intelligent Job Search  • Keyword and natural-language search.  • Filters: location, distance, salary, experience, education, industry, job type, workplace type, company, posting date and remote options.  • Location-aware ranking using geographic distance.  • Personalized recommendations based on the candidate profile.  • Search results should prioritize relevance and proximity rather than random job ordering.  6. AI Job Matching & Eligibility  • Job match score based on skills, experience, education, location, salary and preferences.  • Explainable match breakdown such as Skills 95%, Experience 100%, Education 90%, Location 85%.  • Separate   Eligibility   from   Match Score .  • Mandatory requirements can be configured by employers.  • Examples: required university, degree, major, minimum experience, certification or other lawful job requirements.  • When strict mandatory requirements are enabled, candidates who fail them cannot apply.  • An ineligible candidate should show 'Not Eligible' rather than an arbitrary low match percentage.  7. Recommended Technical Architecture  Mobile:   Flutter for Android/iOS.  Web:   Next.js for public website, candidate web experience and employer/admin interfaces.  Backend:   Laravel API for authentication, business logic, recruitment workflows, payments, permissions and integrations.  Primary database:   PostgreSQL.  Geospatial:   PostGIS for distance and location-aware job ranking.  Vector/semantic matching:   pgvector initially; dedicated search infrastructure can be added later.  Cache/queues:   Redis.  Object storage:   S3-compatible storage for CVs, certificates, course media and other files.  Search at scale:   OpenSearch can be introduced when job/candidate search volume requires it.  Analytics at scale:   ClickHouse can be introduced later for large event datasets.  Architecture principle: deterministic backend rules for hard eligibility, distance, salary and application state; ML/recommendation for relevance; LLM for natural-language interaction, explanations and career assistance.  33.
